Rules

Six rules that keep it Langfuse.

01One highlight per slideThe yellow marks the one idea to remember. Never two colors of highlight.
02Square corners2 px radius at most. Pills are the only round shape.
03Mono for metadataDates, paths, counts, slide numbers and labels are Geist Mono.
04Real artifactsCode, paths, facts and numbers come from the repo or the running stack. Mock-ups say so.
05Dark slide = turning pointUse the ink background only for the gap, a handover, or the call to action.
06Every feature, one Orbisa useNo feature appears without the Orbisa example that shows why it matters.
