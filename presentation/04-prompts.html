prompts/00-working-agreement.md · the seven rules
1We decide, you propose. 2–3 options, trade-offs, a recommendation. Then stop.
2Plan before code. Five bullets, wait for "go".
3Show the proof. A command, a file, or a place in Langfuse.
4Ask, don't assume. Ambiguity becomes a question.
5Write decisions down in decisions/.
6Plain language. Several of us are not native speakers.
7Don't rewrite Helix. Make it observable, prove whether it works.

Pairing with an agent

Habits that keep the team in charge.

1Plan, then "go"Five bullets before any change. Say no early, it's cheap.
2Ground in docslangfuse.com/docs pages end in .md for plain text. Never guess a signature.
3Proof, not "done"An API call, a test run, a click path in the UI.
4Small incrementsOne increment, one gate. Easy to undo, easy to explain.
5Decisions in filesdecisions/ is the team's memory, not the chat.
6Fail open, alwaysPull the Langfuse keys at every gate. The agent must still answer.
