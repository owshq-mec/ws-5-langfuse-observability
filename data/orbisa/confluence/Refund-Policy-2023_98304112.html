Orbisa Wiki : Refund Policy (2023)

Created by Priya Raman, last modified by Priya Raman on Feb 01, 2023

Policy ID: POL-007   Version: 1   Owner: Finance   Effective: February 1, 2023

Scope

All Orbisa subscriptions (Starter, Growth, Enterprise).

Rules

  1. Annual subscriptions can be refunded pro-rata if the customer asks within 30 days of the renewal date.
  2. After 30 days, annual subscriptions are not refundable.
  3. Monthly subscriptions are never refunded. The customer can cancel before the next billing date.

Approval

Every refund must be approved by Finance (the CFO) before Support confirms anything to the customer.
