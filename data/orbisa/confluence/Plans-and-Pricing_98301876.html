Orbisa sells two products: Orbisa Track (shipment tracking API and dashboard) and Orbisa Bill (freight billing and audit). Both are sold in three plans.

PlanList priceBillingUptime SLA
StarterUSD 500 per monthMonthly or annualNone
GrowthUSD 2,400 per monthAnnualNone
EnterpriseCustom, from USD 12,000 per monthAnnual99.9% monthly, see POL-012

We do not sell any other plan. Requests for "Platinum" or "Premium" tiers come from old Brisklane price lists and should be quoted as Enterprise.

Annual plans auto-renew for 12 months unless the contract says otherwise. Discounts follow the Discount Approval Matrix.
