Retention periods

DataRetention
Shipment tracking events (Orbisa Track)24 months
Invoices and freight audit records (Orbisa Bill)7 years
Support tickets36 months

Brisklane customers

From January 1, 2026, the 24-month tracking retention applies to all customers, including customers migrated from Brisklane. Before that date, Brisklane customers kept Brisklane's 12-month retention. Decision recorded in #data-platform on November 4, 2025.
