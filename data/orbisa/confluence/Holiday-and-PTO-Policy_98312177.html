Orbisa Wiki : Holiday and PTO Policy

Created by Marina Costa, last modified by Aisha Bello on Jan 05, 2026

Everyone at Orbisa gets 25 days of paid time off per year, plus local public holidays (Austin or Lisbon).

  • Request PTO at least two weeks in advance for absences longer than three days.
  • Unused PTO carries over up to 5 days into the next year.
  • Account owners must name a backup for their customers before going on leave.
