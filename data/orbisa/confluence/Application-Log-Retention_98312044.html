Engineering standard for logs produced by Orbisa services. This page does not cover customer data; see the Data Retention Policy for that.

Log typeRetention
Application debug logs30 days
Access logs90 days
Security audit logs13 months

Logs are shipped to the central log store and deleted automatically after the retention period.
