Policy ID: POL-007   Version: 2   Owner: Finance and RevOps   Effective: March 1, 2026   Replaces: Refund Policy (2023)

Standard terms (Orbisa paper)

  1. Annual subscriptions on any plan get a full refund if the customer asks within 14 days of the renewal date.
  2. After 14 days, annual subscriptions are not refundable. Service problems are handled with service credits under POL-012.
  3. Monthly subscriptions are never refunded.

Exceptions

  • Contract terms win. If a signed contract gives the customer different refund rights, follow the contract.
  • Brisklane legacy contracts keep Brisklane's refund terms until their first renewal on Orbisa paper.

Approval

Every refund is approved by RevOps. Finance only processes the payout after RevOps approves.
