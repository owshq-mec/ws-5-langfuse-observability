Welcome to Orbisa engineering. Your first week:

  1. Get access to GitHub, the cloud console and the on-call tool from your manager.
  2. Run the tracking stack locally with docker compose and send a test shipment event.
  3. Shadow one on-call shift. On-call rotates weekly; SEV-1 incidents page the primary and the CTO.
  4. Ship a small change to production by Friday. Deploys go through #eng-deploys.

Ask questions in #eng. Nobody expects you to know everything.
