Orbisa Wiki : INC-2304 Postmortem — Carrier webhook TLS expiry

Created by Ingrid Holm, last modified by Ingrid Holm on Aug 29, 2026
IncidentINC-2304
SeveritySEV-1
DateAugust 27, 2026, 06:40–08:27 UTC
Duration1 hour 47 minutes
ImpactOrbisa Track API stopped receiving carrier status updates. Tracking requests returned errors for all customers.

Root cause

The TLS certificate on the carrier-webhook gateway expired. Automatic renewal had been disabled during a migration in July and was never turned back on.

Fix

Issued a new certificate and re-enabled automatic renewal.

SLA impact

Together with INC-2291, unplanned Tracking API downtime in August 2026 was 4 hours 59 minutes. August uptime was 99.33%, below the 99.9% Enterprise commitment in POL-012.
