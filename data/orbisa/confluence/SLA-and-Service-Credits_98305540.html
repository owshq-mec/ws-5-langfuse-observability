Uptime commitment

Enterprise customers get a 99.9% monthly uptime commitment for the Orbisa Track API. Uptime is measured per calendar month: (total minutes − minutes of unplanned outage) ÷ total minutes.

Service credits

Monthly uptimeCredit
99.0% to less than 99.9%10% of that month's subscription fee
Less than 99.0%25% of that month's subscription fee

The customer must request the credit within 30 days after the end of the affected month. Credits are applied to the next invoice. Credits are the only remedy for SLA misses unless the customer's contract says otherwise.

Starter and Growth plans have no uptime commitment.
