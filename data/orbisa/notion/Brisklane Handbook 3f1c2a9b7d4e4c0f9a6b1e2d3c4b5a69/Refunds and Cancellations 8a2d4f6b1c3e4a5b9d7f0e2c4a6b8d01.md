Brisklane keeps refunds simple. Happy customers stay longer.

## Refunds

- Any customer can get a **full refund within 60 days** of a renewal date. No questions asked.
- After 60 days we do not refund, but the customer can cancel before the next renewal.
- Refunds are approved by Luca or by the Ops lead on duty.

## Cancellations

- Customers can cancel at any time from the dashboard.
- Cancellation takes effect at the end of the paid period.

## Plans we sell

Brisklane Basic, Brisklane Pro and Brisklane Platinum. Platinum includes a dedicated routing engineer.
